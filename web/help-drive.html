Connect your Google Drive

When you click Share link, your 3D model is saved in your own Google Drive, in a folder called Scic3DViewer. People who open your link see the model; they never get access to your Drive. You only connect once. It takes about one minute and needs no technical knowledge.

What you need: a Google account (a normal Gmail address is fine), internet access, and a little free space in your Drive (15 GB are free on personal accounts).

Step by step

  1. If you have not yet, click Sign in with Google in the Studio and choose your account. (Pro or Enterprise customers: use the Google account with the email your license was issued to; it unlocks automatically.)
  2. In the Studio, click Connect Drive in the top bar. (Clicking Share link before you connect opens the same window.)
  3. In the window that opens, click Connect Google Drive. Your browser goes to a Google page.
  4. Choose your Google account from the list (or sign in). Use the account whose Drive should hold your models.
  5. You may see the warning “Google hasn’t verified this app”. This is normal for small independent sites. Click Advanced (small grey text), then click Go to … (unsafe).
  6. Google shows the permission being requested: “See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app.” That means this site can only touch files it creates itself, never your other files or photos. Tick the box if there is one, then click Continue (or Allow).
  7. You return to the Studio automatically and see “Google Drive connected”. The top-bar button now reads Drive ✓.
That’s it. Now click Share link → Publish. The model is saved in your Drive and you get a link to send to anyone.

Good to know

  • Privacy: the files are private to your Drive. Nobody can open them from Drive; only this site can read them and shows them to people who have your link.
  • Please don’t delete or rename the files inside the Scic3DViewer folder by hand. The matching link would stop working. To remove a link properly, delete it in the Studio; it also removes the file from your Drive.
  • Disconnecting: open Connect Drive → Disconnect. All your existing links stop working until you connect again. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions (same effect).
  • Different Google account: Disconnect, then connect again and choose the other account. Links you made before will need to be published again.
  • Storage: your models use your own Drive space, not the site’s.

Something went wrong?

What you seeWhat to do
“Google Drive is not enabled on this site yet” The person who runs this site has not finished its one-time Google setup. Ask them to do it; you cannot fix this yourself.
Google page: Error 403: access_denied or “app not verified / not allowed” with no Advanced option The site owner still has to allow your account. Send them your Gmail address and ask them to add you (or to publish the Google app).
“Could not connect Google Drive, please try again” Click Connect Google Drive again and finish within a few minutes, pressing Continue on Google’s page. Do not press Cancel or Back.
The warning page has no Advanced link Use a normal browser window with a personal Gmail account (work or school accounts may block it).
“Connect your Google Drive first” when sharing Connect (steps above). Do this too if you removed access at Google.
“Upload to your Google Drive failed” Check your internet connection and that your Drive is not full. If it keeps failing: Connect Drive → Disconnect, then connect again.
Visitors see “This model is temporarily unavailable” Your Drive was disconnected or the file was deleted. Reconnect Drive; if the file is gone, publish the model again.
