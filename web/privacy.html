2. Google user data

Scic3DViewer requests these Google permissions (scopes), and nothing else:

  • openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile (when you sign in): your name and email address. We use them only to identify you, create your session, apply the license issued to your email and show your name in the app.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (asked on the same Google screen as sign-in, and again if you click “Connect Drive”; you may untick it and still use the app, but then you cannot publish links): access to the files and folders the app itself creates in your Drive. We use it only to save, read and delete the models you publish.

If you connect Google Drive we request only the permission drive.file: access to the files and folders this application itself creates (the folder Scic3DViewer and the models inside). We cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. We use this access only to store, read and delete those model files on your behalf. We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising and do not allow humans to read it except where you ask us for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How Google user data is stored, shared and deleted. Your name and email address are stored in our database. The Drive access token is stored encrypted. We do not share Google user data with third parties, except with service providers that run the app for us (hosting, and the email provider if we email you a license) and where the law requires it. We never use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models, and we do not sell it. You can delete it at any time: disconnect Drive (the token is deleted and revoked at Google), delete your projects (the files are deleted from your Drive), or email us to delete your account data.

3. Legal bases (EU/UK users)

  • Contract: signing you in, hosting your links, applying your license.
  • Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention, the activity log, keeping the service running.
  • Consent: connecting Google Drive (you can withdraw it by disconnecting).
  • Legal obligation: keeping records of payments where the law requires it.

4. Who receives data

  • Google (sign-in and Drive storage).
  • Stripe (payments), only if you buy a license.
  • Our email provider, only to deliver license emails to you.
  • Hosting provider of the server we run on.
  • Content delivery networks (jsDelivr, Tailwind CDN, Google static servers): the pages load the 3D engine and styles from these services, so they receive your IP address and browser details when a page opens, as with any website that uses them.

We do not sell personal data and do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

5. International transfers

Google and Stripe may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

6. How long we keep data

  • Sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Drive token: until you disconnect Drive or ask us to delete your data.
  • Shared project records: until you delete the project or your data.
  • Activity log: kept while needed for security and statistics, and removed when you ask us to delete your data.
  • Payment and license records: as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live you may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and you may complain to your data protection authority. Write to {{CONTACT}} from the email address of your account; we reply within 30 days.

You can already do part of this yourself: delete projects in My projects (this also deletes the file from your Drive), disconnect Drive in the Drive window (this deletes our stored token and revokes it at Google), and withdraw access in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Drive tokens are encrypted at rest, link passwords and session tokens are stored only as hashes, the admin area accepts only the verified administrator account, and access to files in your Drive is limited to the narrow permission described above. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will inform you as the law requires.

9. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the Studio.
